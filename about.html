Meet The Artist

Handmade with Love in Churubusco, IN

Welcome to Pressed By Kenzie! I specialize in creating stunning, custom hand-painted press-on nails that rival any salon manicure.

What started as a passion for nail art has grown into a dedicated business providing high-quality, reusable press-ons. Every set is crafted with attention to detail, using premium professional gel polishes to ensure durability and a beautiful finish.

Whether you're looking for a simple, elegant everyday set or an intricate, bold design for a special occasion, I work closely with you to bring your vision to life. My goal is to make every client feel confident and beautiful without the hassle of long salon appointments.
